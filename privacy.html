Wallora Privacy Policy

Wallora is a local iOS app for archiving wallpaper source images and home or lock screen pairing references. This Privacy Policy explains how Wallora handles information on your device.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Wallora mobile application distributed on the App Store. Wallora is designed as an on-device archive. You do not need to create a Wallora cloud account to use the core archive features.

Information stored on your device

When you use Wallora, the following categories of content may be stored locally in the app sandbox:

  • Setup titles, notes, tags, device aliases, and usage date ranges you enter.
  • Photo copies you attach as source, home, lock, or other role images.
  • Optional dictated note text produced when you use speech-to-text.
  • Local preference flags such as onboarding completion and pack credit balances.
  • Verified in-app purchase transaction identifiers needed for local entitlement bookkeeping.

Wallora does not upload your setup photos or notes to a Wallora business server as part of the core archive workflow.

Permissions

Wallora requests system permissions only when you trigger the related action:

  • Camera — when you tap Take Photo to capture a wallpaper or screen reference.
  • Photo Library — when you choose photos for a setup or save a preview image.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — when you start dictation for setup notes.
  • Notifications — a system prompt may appear when you first reach the home tab. Wallora does not assign a notification center delegate for product push workflows and does not rely on notification delivery to operate the archive.

If you deny a permission, features that depend on it stay unavailable while the rest of the app continues to work.

Purchases

Optional consumable gift packs are processed by Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment credentials. Wallora stores purchase results locally so pack credits can be applied on this device.

Analytics and tracking

Wallora does not include App Tracking Transparency advertising tracking in this package. The app does not sell personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete a setup, clear local data in Settings, or remove the app. Clearing local data removes setups, purchase history stored by the app, and preference flags from this installation. Export or share actions you start yourself may create copies outside the app that Wallora cannot revoke.

Children

Wallora is intended for a general audience that can manage personal photos and device settings. Do not submit sensitive information about children through screenshots or notes.

Contact

For privacy questions about Wallora, use the support channel listed on the App Store product page for Wallora.
